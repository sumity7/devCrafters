Questions clients ask us.

How long does a website take?

Starter and business websites are ready in 2–3 days. Production websites and online stores take about 1 week. Bigger custom projects get their own timeline before we start.

What is a production website?

It is a complete website that is live and ready for customers. We take care of the domain, hosting, SSL (https), business email, Google setup and full testing, so you do not have to set up anything yourself.

Why do prices start "from"?

Every business needs something slightly different. The starting price covers the package as listed; extra pages, features or customisation are added to your quote, which you approve before work starts.

Should I choose WooCommerce or Shopify?

Both work well. WooCommerce gives you full control and no monthly platform fee; Shopify is quicker to run with less maintenance. We recommend one after understanding your products, budget and plans.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Every site comes with an easy admin panel, and we walk you through adding products, pages and blog posts at handover.

Which payment methods can my store accept?

Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal, PayU, Cashfree, UPI, cards, net banking and cash on delivery, depending on your business.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can give it a modern design or move it to a better platform while keeping your content, products and search rankings.

What happens after the website goes live?

Every package includes support after launch. After that you can choose monthly maintenance for updates, backups, security and changes.
